Bundled font coverage report
=============================
Date: 2026-09-28
Method: fontTools 4.66.0 (temp venv /tmp/opencode/fontvenv, outside the project).
For every candidate family we downloaded the real woff2 files served by
fonts.googleapis.com (Google Fonts) for weights 400 and 700 of the three
subsets we bundle (latin, latin-ext, vietnamese) and checked that the
cmap union of those files contains every code point in the test strings.

The Yoruba/Igbo dot-below block (U+1EA0-U+1EF1) lives in Google's
"vietnamese" subset for these families, so that subset is required and
is bundled; latin-ext alone is NOT sufficient.

KEPT (all three languages pass: Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, plus punctuation):
  - Noto Sans 400/700      (noto-sans-{latin,latin-ext,vietnamese}-{400,700}.woff2)
  - Noto Serif 400/700     (noto-serif-{latin,latin-ext,vietnamese}-{400,700}.woff2)
  - Montserrat 400/700     (montserrat-{latin,latin-ext,vietnamese}-{400,700}.woff2)
  - Merriweather 400/700   (merriweather-{latin,latin-ext,vietnamese}-{400,700}.woff2)

DROPPED (fail the gate):
  - Open Sans: missing Yoruba U+1E62/U+1E63, all Igbo, all Hausa.
  - Lora: missing Yoruba U+1E62/U+1E63, all Igbo, all Hausa.

Test strings:
  Yoruba:   "ẹ Ẹ ọ Ọ ṣ Ṣ" and "Ẹ̀ṣọ́ ọ̀rọ̀ Ọlọ́run"
            (precomposed letters AND base+combining sequences:
             U+1EB8+U+0300, U+1EE3+U+0301, U+1ECD+U+0301)
  Igbo:     "ị Ị ọ Ọ ụ Ụ ṅ Ṅ"
  Hausa:    "ɓ Ɓ ɗ Ɗ ƙ Ƙ ƴ Ƴ"
  Punct:    "’ ‘ ' \" “ ” — –"
  Basic:    "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only 12345%.$"

Note on combining marks: cmap coverage confirms the combining marks
(U+0300, U+0301, U+0304, U+0323) exist in the bundled files; the visual
mark positioning (GDEF/GPOS anchors) should be eyeballed on the specimen
page at scripts/font-specimen.html (open it through the dev server and
append /static/presentation/fonts/... paths as needed).

Total payload: 24 woff2 files, ~1.6 MB (below the 3 MB target).
Licences (OFL 1.1) per family in LICENSES/.
